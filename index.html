If they say a station

Four rules of the room
  • Do not say “pain.” Say surge, wave, or comfort level.
  • Do not offer medication. She will ask if she wants it.
  • Do not share dilation numbers. Fewer exams.
  • Default is movement and the Jacuzzi. Monica stickers if monitoring is required.

Labor
  • Freedom of movement. Monica sticker monitor if continuous monitoring is required.

  • Birth the placenta naturally. Allow up to 60 minutes.
  • Breast crawl immediately. 1–2 hours of bonding.

Newborn

Seven movements to make room. Hold most for about a minute. Built to Birth.

1 Psoas release

One foot on an elevated surface. Other leg hangs. Hips level. Gently swing the hanging leg forward and back for 1 minute. Switch sides.

2 Tuck + untuck

All fours. Move the pelvis, not the back. Tucking opens the inlet. Untucking opens the outlet. 1 minute.

3 QL stretch

Doorway. Cross right foot over left. Lean left, shoulders square, hands on the frame. Shift hips right and breathe into the ribs. 1 minute. Switch.

4 Tuck, lunge + lean

Kneel, one foot planted in front. Tuck pelvis and shift forward for a front-hip stretch. Raise the same-side arm and lean. 1 minute each side.

5 Elbows on chair

Kneel facing a chair. Elbows on the seat. Spine long, pelvis neutral. 1 minute.

6 Fascia release

Partner jiggles glutes, hips, and thighs with palms. 1–5 minutes.

7 Forward-leaning inversion

Kneel on a couch or low bed. Forearms to the floor, head hanging loose. 1 minute. Come back to kneeling for 30 seconds before getting down.

Spencer: pick one and stay with it through the whole surge. Built to Birth.

1 Light touch massage

Stroke an X across the back, shoulder to low back. Then one stroke down the spine. Repeat.

2 Facial relaxation

Fingertips at the hairline. Stroke the jaw. Circle the jaw muscles. Continue down the jawline and neck.

3 SP6 acupressure

Thumb four finger-widths above the inner ankle bone. Firm pressure on the fleshy part behind the shin, through the surge.

4 Jiggle / fascia release

Palms on the bottom. Gently jiggle hips, glutes, thighs, and calves.

5 Sitz bone counter pressure

Palms on the sitz bones. You press forward as she presses back. Imagine spreading the sitz bones apart.

6 BL32 acupressure

Thumbs four finger-widths above the gluteal crease, slightly left and right. Hold through the surge.

7 Double hip squeeze

Palms on the fleshy outer hips, fingers inward. Squeeze toward each other through the surge.

8 Sacral counter pressure

Stacked palms on the sacrum. Toward the bum opens the top of the pelvis. Toward her back opens the bottom.

9 Destressor breath

Inhale nose 4 seconds. Exhale mouth 6 seconds. Rag-doll the body on every breath.

10 LI4 acupressure

The fleshy mound between thumb and forefinger. Firm pressure through the surge.
